What this audit covers

The audit follows a request from intake through planning, execution and publication, then checks the provider and deployment boundaries that carry the result into GitHub and production. Conditional risks stay separate from confirmed defects so their uncertainty remains visible.

The clearest user-facing break is image evidence: an upload can succeed and still return 404, remain download-only, or vanish from the saved submission (WEB-01–WEB-04). Even a saved attachment currently gives planning and execution no usable image reference (WF-09).

Intake and evidence→Planning and approval→Execution and validation→Review/publication→Provider and production

Intended behavior: preserve each declared upload, validate and claim it atomically, show it to the administrator, and pass a controlled read-only reference into planning and execution. Approved work should retain its evidence and exact commit while every downstream stage reports truthful, recoverable state.

Recommended repair order

  1. Restore the complete image-evidence flow. Make uploads retrievable, visible on tickets, atomically validated/claimed, and available through controlled read-only references to planning and execution (WEB-01–WEB-05, WF-09).
  2. Restore worker progress and truthful cancellation. Clear the AI provider dispatch gate, cancellation races, unbounded validation, stream-write failures, shutdown classification, queued-attempt reconciliation, exposed-run cancellation, and the canonical ticket cancellation path (WF-01, WF-03–WF-05, WF-08, WF-10, WF-11, WEB-08).
  3. Bind deployment decisions to their evidence. Make timestamps, workflow selection, branch SHAs, registry checks, migrations, artifacts and retries agree about what was actually checked and deployed (INT-01–INT-03, INT-07–INT-09, OPS-1, OPS-3–OPS-5).
  4. Make operator truth and form boundaries reliable. Add sign-out and real worker health, validate published forms and direct edits, and repair the remaining ticket UI boundaries (WEB-06, WEB-07, WEB-09).
  5. Make test safety and coverage trustworthy. Isolate the E2E wrapper before relying on it, then repair fresh-database seeding and CI coverage; review the conditional dev-tool dependency exposure (WEB-10 → WEB-11/WEB-12, WEB-13).
  6. Reconcile state and preserve operator evidence. Repair linked-ticket state, notification diagnostics, import/deactivation behavior, publication retries and busy-worker maintenance (INT-04–INT-06, WF-06, WF-07, OPS-2, OPS-6, OPS-7).
  7. Keep conditional risks visible. Decide policy and add targeted validation before promoting follow-up items to confirmed defects.
